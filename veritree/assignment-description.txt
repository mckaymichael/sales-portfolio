Overview

You're going to pick a company you'd actually want to work for, and then prove you belong there. This assignment is about showing you can do the research, tailor your work to a real audience, and walk into a room ready to make a case for yourself. You'll research a target company inside and out, build a mini-portfolio that speaks directly to what they need, and pitch it to the class like it's the real thing.

Instructions

Pick a specific dream company. Dig into who they are: their culture, values, recent projects, the kind of clients they work with (if applicable), and what they're actually hiring for. Then build a customised mini-portfolio that makes it obvious why you'd be a great fit. Not obvious to you. Obvious to them.

Frame your presentation as a real scenario: maybe you're interviewing for a full-time role, pitching yourself as a freelancer, or going after a specific project. Choose 1–2 portfolio pieces that best demonstrate your alignment with that company, and be ready to explain why you chose each one and how your skills address something they actually need.

You will have a strict 4 minute timer for presentations. When the timer goes, you have to stop talking. Practice, practice, practice.

Your presentation format should match the job – if it's a motion graphics job, show a reel; if you're in product design, bring prototypes. Make it feel like you. After your pitch, your classmates (AKA the client/company) will give you real feedback on whether they'd hire you.

Deliverables

In-Class Mini-Portfolio Presentation (5 minutes): Your strategic pitch in whatever format fits your discipline, followed by a Q&A with your classmates acting as company reps.
